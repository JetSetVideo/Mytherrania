## What changed

- `src/game/Agility.ts` (new): `agilityFactors(agility)` -> `{ animSpeed, moveSpeed, dodge, recovery, turnRate, dash }`, 1.0 at sheet agility 10, monotone, clamped; `effectiveAgility(stats)` = runtime agility + SHEET_OFFSET − body-mass drag.
- Agility is wired into: MotionStyle (wind-up ÷ animSpeed, recovery/settle ÷ recovery, overshoot trimmed) and through it every SwingDriver phase; Engine `#speedMul` (player and bots), the player's turn response, every skill lunge/dash/roll, the truce roam, a cast's pushback; MatchSim's step, lunge and wind-up; `Stats.attackDamage` (a deterministic graze, so Engine, MatchSim, ArenaHarness and BattleCorpus share it); the attack cadence (a small share in `Stats.shape` and `Races.statDelta`); the five mobility/evasion powers (`Skills.AGILITY_POWERS`) and every power's recovery tail.
- Side edges: body mass drags agility (strength, vitality, resilience); `PowerCost.castFocus` (focus + ¼ intelligence) feeds the channel, `PowerCost.manaIntelligence` (intelligence + ¼ focus) the mana discount — in derivePower AND the live cast.
- The Serpent birthsign is supple: `{ reflex: 1, focus: 1, agility: 1 }` (was reflex 2, focus 1).
- Feet: `FootTurnout.ts` (new), `Skeleton.legRoll.toeOut`, `AnimatorOutput.toeOut`, `Rig.legFK3(..., toeOut)` / `Measure.footWorld(..., toeOut)`, the Animator's ankle target; the turn-out is a yaw about the vertical through the ankle.
- `src/game/Influence.ts` (new): the data-only INFLUENCES graph; `tests/wave_x_influence_test.ts` proves every edge.
- POWER_CALIBRATION re-fitted with `tools/tune-balance.ts` (25 rounds x 3 seeds x 3000 fights/pair).

## Reflex vs agility (the split)

| | reflex = REACTION | agility = BODY SPEED and EVASION |
|---|---|---|
| swing | strike phase, kinetic chain lag, most of the cadence (−0.02 s/point) | wind-up, recovery, settle, overshoot; a small cadence share (−0.006 s/point) |
| combat | critical chance | graze on incoming blows (dodge) |
| movement | — | top speed, turn response, dash/roll/lunge distance |
| techniques | cooldowns; reaction-scaled powers (Backstab, Piercing Shot…) | Whirlwind, Sprint, Vanish, Shadow Sprint, Killing Spree; every power's recovery tail; cast pushback |

## The scale

Runtime `CombatStats.agility` (Stats.ts PRIMARY 3..9 + race/sign deltas + spent points) sits on a different scale from the Leveling sheet (CLASS_BASE ~8..16). Sheet = runtime + 6 (per class the difference is 5..7). Body-mass drag: 0.05 sheet points per kg above 70 kg (Physics.bodyFromStats mass).

## Honest gaps

- **Render hookup for the toe-out is NOT in lane L's files.** `Skeleton.legRoll.toeOut` is set and the game-side FK (Rig/Measure/Animator) uses it, but `src/graphics/body/SkinnedHumanoid.ts` (lane B) must multiply `Ry(toeYaw)` innermost onto the ankle bone (after the pitch and roll) for it to be visible; the patch is in `artifacts/messages-wave-x-L.md`. Until then the screen shows straight feet.
- Toe-out is 9°, not 10°: at 10° the ±15° cross-fall pin failed by a hair (planted sole 10.2 vs 10 mm off the slope); at 9° every planted-foot band holds. Walking stance slide 2.4 -> 2.7 mm (bands are 20 mm).
- The turn-out is a yaw about the VERTICAL through the ankle (sole heights and pitch exactly unchanged). Two earlier attempts failed and are recorded: about the shin (outermost) slid a walking ball 2.4 -> 8.4 mm; about the foot's up axis (innermost) leaked the guard's lateral tilt into sole pitch (0.004° -> 0.71° on the real rig). The Animator's IK places the ankle so the TURNED-OUT ball lands on the planted spot (`Animator.#ankleTarget`); `tools/anim-report.ts` (not in lane L's table) was given the one-token `out.toeOut[i]` so its measurement sees the same foot.
- No browser probe was run for lane L; the evidence is pure-module tests (arithmetic + Animator/SwingDriver/MatchSim behaviour). Live wiring (Engine `#speedMul`, turn response, lunge, pushback, cast focus) is by code path, shared functions with MatchSim, not observed in Chrome.
- Turn rate only affects the PLAYER's walk response: bots and MatchSim face their rival instantly, so there is no turn to speed up there.
- The dodge is a deterministic graze (damage ÷ 1 + 0.25·(dodge − 1)), not a miss roll: adding an rng draw would have re-seeded every harness. It is priced into the class solve (Stats.deriveCombatStats ehp, powerIndex), so archetypes stay at DPS x eHP parity.
- Race balance (elf/dwarf/ork arena) was already far from 50 % at HEAD (dwarf/ork mean |dev| 9-11 %); the tuner fits the human arena only, and this lane did not re-balance races.
- The headless MatchSim casters (Mage/Necromancer/Priest) were already below 45 % at HEAD; agility (low on casters) does not fix that, castFocus helps a little.
- `tools/gen-spec-tables.ts` still prints the column header `WIL` (not owned by lane L; reported in messages).
