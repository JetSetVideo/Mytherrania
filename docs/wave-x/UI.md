## What changed

- **`src/ui/ValueTone.ts`** (C-TONE): `toneFor(value, {neutral, span, surface, invert})` → `rgb(...)`; green
  above neutral, red below, warm grey at it, ease-out ramp saturating at `neutral ± span`. Two ink sets (walnut /
  parchment) because one colour cannot reach 4.5:1 on both; `paintTones(root)` reads `data-tone*` attributes and
  writes `style.color` through CSSOM (CSP-safe), picking the parchment inks inside `.stepper` / field wells.
  `signed()`, `contrastRatio()`, `toneAttrs()`.
- **Toned numbers**: creator sheet + hero card sheet (attributes and derived numbers by their deviation from the
  all-class × all-race mean, the attack interval inverted; hand-tweak deltas `(+2)` by their sign), race detail
  ranges (median vs the mean of the races), sub-race/birthsign modifiers, History stat cells (each attribute by its
  gap to the other fighter), the attribute steppers' parchment wells (change vs the rolled base), the inspect
  card (`tone-good`/`tone-bad` + `toneValue`; CSS selectors fixed), combat text (every floating number is a loss:
  the negative hue; misses/absorbs neutral).
- **Forged-steel sign** (`steelSignHtml`): first child of the Creator's viewer box (`.preview-stage`) and of the
  hero card's figure; hammered (generated feTurbulence + diffuse-lighting SVG, the `--u-tex-*` approach) and
  brushed steel, bevelled rim, four domed rivets, engraved race + sub-race. The old banner under the hero
  portrait is gone. PreviewScene's API is untouched (DOM only).
- **Agility + Focus**: the sheet has seven primaries; race detail adds Agility; History cells read
  VIT RES FOC REF STR INT AGI (the old `W`/no-agility cell is gone); UiTree says "seven attributes … agility".
- **Creator** (`CreatorRoll.ts`, new `CreatorTweak.ts`, `Menu.ts`): names from `generateName(rng, race, sub-race,
  sex)` (a child stream, so every legacy per-seed draw survives — pitfall 11), sex + `rollAge` on the roll;
  `rerollPart` for name / ancestry / class / body / face / age / stats / sign (a labelled dice beside each);
  `withAncestry` keeps the body's place in the band, the life fraction, the attribute changes, and regenerates
  an untyped name in the new phonology (a typed name is locked). Age stepper (bounds `Age.ageBounds`), height /
  weight (race band), seven attribute steppers (floor = base − 3, cap = base + `PER_STAT_CAP`, total ≤
  `budgetForLevel(level)`, "N of M points left"), seven silhouette steppers (race value ± 9 %, ≤ `SILHOUETTE_TOP`).
  Every control commits on `input`; the sheet re-renders at once (`tweakedStats` = rolled + `statDelta(now) −
  statDelta(base)`), and the 3D model is refreshed on the next animation frame (`onRoll`, coalesced).
- **Centring**: hero tabs (symmetric padding, centred content) and the stepper well (one grid row when the
  sub-label is empty); the arena pickers wrap before a well gets narrower than its label.

## Honest gaps

- **Age and silhouette edits do not change the 3D model** (table above): `PreviewScene.previewKey` omits them and
  `buildPreviewRig` overrides the silhouette with a name-seeded one (lane B's files). The controls, the clamps and
  the profile are right; the request is in `artifacts/messages-wave-x-U.md`.
- **Latency**: the debounce is one frame (rAF wait above), a warm rebuild ~45–55 ms, but the first two rebuilds
  after an ancestry change cost ~0.7 s (cache warm-up inside `buildPreviewRig`, lane B).
- **The fight does not yet use hand-spent attribute points for the human player** (Engine applies a spend in
  spectate only); the sheet shows them through the game's own `statDelta` law. Request filed to lane L.
- Two pins outside lane U still expect six attributes (`tests/menu_creator_test.ts:133`, `tests/spec_test.ts`
  SPEC LL3) — they are the failures listed above; the parent updates them.
- The inspect card's smooth ramp needs the engine to render `data-tone` (it renders `tone-good/bad` classes,
  which are toned now). Combat text uses the negative hue in CSS; its exact-string tests were left untouched.
- One point of Strength may move no derived number (statDelta floors strength / 2) — that is the game's law,
  shown faithfully; the probe steps +2.
- Centring was fixed where centring is the intent; lists and space-between rows (race tiles, class rows, sheet
  columns, skill-card tops) are reported but intentionally not centred.
