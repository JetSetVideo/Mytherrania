## What changed

- **Hero reroll** (`src/ui/HeroReroll.ts`, `Menu.ts`): one stone dice button **Reroll hero** in the hero bar. `rerollHero(prev, rng)` =
  the Creator's own seeded `rollProfile` (new ancestry, calling, sign, palette, body, face, age and a name in the new phonology),
  keeping the hero's **level** and **equipment**. No modal: an **Undo** button appears beside it until another hero is selected.
  Persistence goes through the engine like rename: the Menu exposes `heroReplacement` and emits `onProfileAction("reroll", id)`;
  lane P's `case "reroll"` writes `heroRecordPatch(character)` with `ProfileRepo.update` (same id, record, keybindings, telemetry).
  If an engine without that case ignores it, the bar says "Reroll not saved…" instead of offering an Undo of nothing.
- **Level medallion** (`levelBadgeHtml`): a struck copper coin (`.plate-copper`) left of the name in the hero bar, a small coin on each hero tab
  (the old "· lv N" text is gone).
- **Hero bar** (`.hero-bar.frame-steel`): the stats column's head — medallion + name/meta on the left, Reroll / Undo / Duplicate / Rename /
  Play this hero pushed to the right edge; Delete stays apart at the foot of the column. Phones: the verbs take their own row, 2 per line.
- **Titles centred on both axes**: screen titles, panel heads (hint on its own centred line), sheet/group/skill/digest heads, the picker
  title, the gilded MYTHERRANIA (now also sized to fit its page: 333 px of text overflowed a 236 px box at 320 px) and the Settings title
  (close seal pinned right, title centred over the dialog).
- **Design system** (styles.css "Wave Y" block): named tokens as equations of the type scale (`--frame-w = max(2px, .2·t4)`,
  `--frame-w-lg = max(3px, .22·t3)`, `--bevel = .6·frame-w`, `--radius-plate = .45·t4`, `--radius-win = .7·t3`,
  `--socket = clamp(44px, 2.9·t3, 64px)`), metal gradient stacks `--metal-steel / -bronze / -copper` (noise layer: brushed lines or hammered
  dimples, a sheen, a 6-stop base ramp), `--stone` (grain + speckle + mottle + granite ramp), and the recipes `.frame-steel`,
  `.frame-bronze`, `.plate-copper`, `.btn-stone`. Applied: windows + Settings steel, panels bronze, the primary act (Enter the ring,
  Play this hero) stone, the dice stone-on-violet, medallions copper. HUD `.pool-bar` (C-HUD): steel frame, dim potential, bright current,
  hatched lost region; inside a HUD panel it is a compact strip aligned under its pool's squared bar.
- **Equipment sockets** (`src/game/EquipmentCatalog.ts`, `src/ui/EquipIcons.ts`, `Menu.ts`): the viewer canvas stands between
  five sockets (helmet, armour, gloves, belt, legs) and four (weapon, off hand, cape, boots). A socket opens a picker over the viewer
  (close: ✕, Esc or gamepad B through `Menu.back()`). Armour/off-hand lists are greyed "coming soon" placeholders (disabled; `equip()`
  refuses them). Weapons = every real archetype (17, all legal for every calling — the game has no class restriction) + "Class default".
- **Weapon socket**: shows the wielded archetype's icon (17 generated line icons, one per archetype, plus 8 slot glyphs); choosing a
  weapon writes `profile.weaponType` through the same route as the weapon table, the table selection follows and the 3D preview rebuilds
  via `onRoll`. Fixed on the way: picking "Class default" in the weapon TABLE never refreshed the preview.

## Mouse click paths

- **Reroll a saved hero**: title → **Fighters** → (the hero's tab) → in the bar right of the name: **Reroll hero** (dice). **Undo** appears
  beside it; one click restores the previous hero. Gamepad/keyboard: Tab to the button, Enter.
- **Level**: Fighters → the copper coin left of the hero's name (and the small coin on each tab).
- **Duplicate / Rename / Play this hero**: Fighters → hero tab → the right end of the bar over the statistics. **Delete**: foot of the column.
- **Equipment**: Fighters → **+ New** tab → Preview panel → click any socket beside the model → the list opens over the viewer
  (greyed "coming soon" pieces cannot be chosen) → **✕** (or Esc / gamepad B) closes it.
- **Weapon**: Fighters → **+ New** → the gold-framed **Weapon** socket (right of the model, top) → click an icon (e.g. Maul) → the socket
  icon, the caption "Weapon: Maul", the Calling ▸ Weapon table and the 3D model all change. **Class default** (first tile) goes back.

## Honest gaps

- **Armour is placeholder data**: 8 sockets × 3 named future pieces, all `available: false`; nothing is equipped or drawn on the model.
- **`profile.equip` is not persisted by the library**: `ProfileLibrary.ts` (no lane owns it) stores `weaponType` but not `equip`; the reroll
  patch carries `equip`, but `toCharacter` drops it on read. Request R2 in `artifacts/messages-wave-y-U.md`. `EquipmentSlots` also lacks
  `offhand` (stored structurally as an extra key).
- **A rerolled hero's attributes** are the new calling's level-1 starting allocation (rollProfile); the level is kept, spent points of a
  higher-level hero are not re-spent.
- The weapon socket is on the generator only; the saved hero card shows the weapon in its meta line, not as a socket.
- Title centring is measured on the titles listed above; disclosure rows (`.opt-head`, chevron right) and list rows are not titles and stay
  left-aligned. The worst after-value is a script-face h2 at 0.98 px vertical (inside the 1 px gate, but close).
- The HUD pool-bar look was measured on a specimen with lane P's class contract; in the live fight it renders under each squared bar
  (`artifacts/qa-wave-y/ui/audit/fight-*.png`), so the HUD now shows two meters per pool (lane P's design choice; the strip is compact).
