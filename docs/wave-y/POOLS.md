# Wave Y — lane P: pool POTENTIAL (health, mana/Magicka, stamina) and the pool-aware AI

> GENERATED by `deno run -A tools/gen-wave-y-pools.ts` from `src/game/Potential.ts`, `artifacts/wave-y-P-balance.json` and `artifacts/qa-wave-y/pools/report.json`. Do not edit numbers by hand.

## The law (C-POT)

Every pool is `{ max, potential, current }` with `0 <= current <= potential <= max`. `max` is the sheet; `potential` is what the fighter can still recover to this match; regen, grants and every heal (blessing, lifesteal, health orb) refill `current` only up to `potential`. The potential falls through three channels and recovers slowly at rest:

1. **Wounds** (health): `loss = WOUND_SHARE · damage · woundFactor`, `woundFactor = clamp(1 + WOUND_STRENGTH_PER_POINT·(attacker.strength − 5) − WOUND_GUARD_PER_POINT·((vitality + resilience)/2 − 5))`. Every blow, skill DoT, burn/poison tick and wall impact.
2. **Enemy effects** (mana, stamina): while a harmful status holds, `STATUS_DRAIN[status][pool] · max` per second.
3. **Fatigue** (mana, stamina) from spending: over a rolling window, `amount` (spent in FATIGUE_WINDOW s, share of max), `rate` (spent in RATE_WINDOW s ÷ RATE_WINDOW, share/s) and `frequency` (spends in FATIGUE_WINDOW s). `overload = Σ max(0, x − free)/free`; each spend loses `min(cost, cost · FATIGUE_SHARE · overload / resist)`; `resist(stamina) = clamp(1 + 0.04·(vitality − 5)) · race endurance`, `resist(mana) = clamp(1 + 0.05·(focus − 5))`.
4. **Rest**: after REST_DELAY s without a spend / blow / drain on that pool, `+REST_RATE[pool] · max` per second, never above max. Never below `POTENTIAL_FLOOR[pool] · max` (the stamina floor sits above Resources' exhaustion wake fraction, so exhaustion always lifts — SPEC Y9).

The SAME functions run in `src/engine/Engine.ts` (live fight) and `src/balance/MatchSim.ts` (headless): `fighterPotential`, `woundPotential`, `recordSpend`, `statusDrain`, `tickPotential`, `withPotentials`, `capOf`.

## Constants

| constant | value | meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `WOUND_SHARE` | `0.2` | share of a blow that becomes lost health potential (neutral matchup) |
| `WOUND_STRENGTH_PER_POINT` | `0.04` | wound factor per attacker strength point above 5 |
| `WOUND_GUARD_PER_POINT` | `0.03` | wound factor removed per point of mean(vitality, resilience) above 5 |
| `WOUND_FACTOR_CLAMP` | `[0.5,1.6]` | bounds of the wound factor |
| `FATIGUE_WINDOW` | `8 s` | window of the amount and frequency measures |
| `RATE_WINDOW` | `2 s` | window of the burst-rate measure |
| `FREE_AMOUNT` | `0.4` | spend free of fatigue per window (share of max) |
| `FREE_RATE` | `0.15 /s` | burst rate free of fatigue (share of max per s) |
| `FREE_COUNT` | `3` | spends free of fatigue per window |
| `FATIGUE_SHARE` | `0.5` | share of an over-paced spend lost at overload 1 |
| `RESIST_PER_POINT` | `{"stamina":0.04,"mana":0.05}` | fatigue resistance per point of vitality (stamina) / focus (mana) above 5 |
| `RESIST_CLAMP` | `[0.6,1.8]` | bounds of the attribute part of the resistance |
| `STATUS_DRAIN` | `{"stunned":{"stamina":0.01,"mana":0.01},"hypnotised":{"mana":0.02},"controlled":{"mana":0.02},"burning":{"stamina":0.012},"poisoned":{"stamina":0.015},"chilled":{"stamina":0.015},"slowed":{"stamina":0.01}}` | per-second drain while an enemy status holds (share of max) |
| `REST_DELAY` | `3 s` | quiet time before a pool's potential recovers |
| `REST_RATE` | `{"health":0.002,"mana":0.006,"stamina":0.008}` | recovery per second at rest (share of max) |
| `POTENTIAL_FLOOR` | `{"health":0.25,"mana":0.4,"stamina":0.4}` | the potential never falls below this share of max |

## The pool-aware AI (C-AI)

`CombatView.self` / `CombatView.enemy` (DecisionTree.FighterKnowledge) carry all seven attributes, the three pools `{max, potential, current}`, the exhaustion flag and the fatigue overload of both fighters — filled by `AiBrain.fighterKnowledge` in Engine.#botFight and MatchSim alike. Two decisions read them:

- **press** (`AiBrain.pressDecision`, wrapped round the tree by `poolAwareTree`): an enemy that is exhausted, winded (stamina now ≤ 0.35), fatigued (stamina potential ≤ 0.9) or wounded (health potential ≤ 0.94) while self is no worse off -> strike when ready in reach, close in when out of reach (overrides block/retreat; never with the back to an open edge).
- **pace / conserve** (`AiBrain.poolWiseSkill`): skip a technique whose spend would burn more than 2 % of a pool's max in fatigue (casters do not overspend mana into fatigue), and hold stamina spends while own stamina potential < 0.6 with stamina now < 50 % — unless the enemy is under 0.25 hp (finisher).

Measured (`PotentialStudy.awareStudy`): 3000 design points, each played twice (seat A aware vs the previous brain, then seat B), counting only matchups where both seats consult a brain (strategy basic/full). A no-op control (every aware decision switched off) scores exactly 0.5 by construction.

|---|---|---|---|

**Effect: none measurable.** The shipped variant scores 0.4946 ± 0.0098 — within 1 SE of 0.5. Ring-outs decide 65.1 % of matches, and the potentials fall slowly (see the table above), so the press fires mostly where the tree already attacks and the conserve gate rarely binds. Two harsher variants were tried during development and rejected on measurement (1 500 points each): pressing also when not ready (advance into the enemy) scored 0.479 ± 0.014, a retreat-to-pace rule 0.496 ± 0.014.

**NeuralPolicy** keeps its 6 inputs (`FEATURES = 6`, 6 -> 8 -> 5): its weights are trained/evolved against that layout (artifacts/neural.json, NeuralTrainer, EvolutionHarness), so widening the input would invalidate every stored network. The Brain's hybrid mode still sees the potentials through the tree it falls back on (`poolAwareTree`).

## Honest gaps

- **MatchSim models no statuses**, so the status channel there is only the stun (a stun > 0.6 s drains like the engine's `stunned` status). Burn/poison/hypnosis drains and DoT wounds exist in the live engine only.
- **Health has no natural regeneration** in this game; the health potential therefore only caps heals (blessing, lifesteal, health orb). Its balance effect is small by construction.
- **The AI effect is null within ±1 SE** (see above). The decisions are wired and unit-tested (SPEC Y12), but they do not yet win more fights.
- **The HUD shows two bars per pool** until lane U decides: the legacy FighterPanel bar (now fed the real potential as its dark fill) and the new `.pool-bar` sibling. See artifacts/messages-wave-y-P.md.
- **A Creator tweak of the allocation is not persisted by ProfileLibrary** (an unowned file): the seven attributes reload at the hero's ancestry/calling values (SPEC Y13 passes), but +agility/+focus tweaks are lost. Patch and the ready test (SPEC Y13b, ignored) are in artifacts/messages-wave-y-P.md.
- The fatigue law is steep for bursts (4 powers in 4.5 s cost a Mage a large share of its mana potential, see the worked examples) but skill cooldowns make such bursts rare in play; the mean fatigue per match is small (table above).
- The RATE measure counts a single large spend as a burst: one 24-mana power is 0.375 of a Warrior's 64-mana pool in one instant, i.e. 0.19 /s over RATE_WINDOW (> FREE_RATE 0.15), so a low-intelligence fighter loses ~3 mana potential per power even when paced (worked examples). Intended as "too much at once", but it is a design choice to revisit.
- The probe's player seat stands idle (no input), so its wounds come from one side; the opponent's own potentials are read but not driven.
