## Honest gaps

- **The mesh is coarse (~4.2–4.5 k vertices, 2.5 cm cells).** The edge highlight lands mostly on hands/feet/shoulders/knuckles; sub-cell edges (brows, lips) are out of reach and the face/ears stay skipped (FORM_SKIP), as in wave V.
- **The cavity wash is a broad-scale depth, not a ray-traced AO.** On a mostly convex body the washed vertices are LESS convex than average (mean H about half the body's), not concave on average — SPEC G63 pins that measured relation, not more.
- **Contrast gain is modest (+0.07–0.10 over wave V)**: the wave-V `noise mush` pin (band-sized jumps ≤ +1 pp on the arms) bounds how hard the wash can be; the arms read 1.50 % vs the 1.59 % cap.
- **Chiaroscuro is in BIND-pose height**: a lying body keeps its standing gradient (the paint is a vertex attribute).
- **Live wiring is K's line** (`SkinnedHumanoid.ts`: `buildFaceParts(..., appearance, bodySpec.stats)`, done in this wave); the Chrome heads below come through the Creator's `buildPreviewRig(profile, stats)` path.
- **A style can switch with the length**: the ponytail variant of flowing hair needs a cut > 1.2 head heights, so a low intelligence can turn a ponytail into loose flowing hair (human young, I=0 below). The tier never goes DOWN as I rises (SPEC G68).
- **Shader sub-strand grey** (`uGreySub` = `stats.greySub`) mixes toward the grey colour on the GPU; for a near-white blond that grey can be darker than the pigment. The CPU card colours are pinned never-darker per channel; the shader's sub-strands are not.
- r2 -> r3 (Q2 FAIL Z-35, 'silver cap'): the intelligence share first turned extra WHOLE clumps grey and lightened the scalp underlayer, so the lit crown read as a silver helmet; then a braid blend made the braid solid silver. r3 spreads the extra share evenly (shader sub-strands + tip silvering) and keeps the underlayer at the age colour; image-model check of `int-elf-young-1000.png` / `int-human-young-1000.png`: 'one head of hair with silver streaks through its length'.
- Crop / mohawk / shaved styles cut their own length: intelligence changes their density and silver, not their tier.
- The garment's dark belly blotches seen by the image model are present BEFORE the pass too (wave-X abs/cloth detail + cel bands), not introduced here.
