Every number in the tables below is generated by `deno run -A tools/measure-wave-gen-anatomy.ts` from

## What changed

- **`src/graphics/body/StatMorph.ts` (new, pure)** — `statMorph(stats, race, subrace, sex): Morph`. Each stat is
  normalised piecewise-linearly around `STAT_REFERENCE` (lane P's StatScale: 500) to u ∈ [−1, 1] (clamped; NaN = 0);
  every term is `1 + up·u` (u ≥ 0) / `1 + down·u` (u < 0): identity at the reference, monotone, bounded
  (`morphBounds()`), no side term (left = right).
  - strength → girth of pectoralis (+30/−15 %), deltoid (+28/−14), latissimus (+30/−10), trapezius (+30/−15),
    biceps (+32/−15), quadriceps (+25/−12), calf (+22/−12); bone density (clavicle, knee and elbow condyles,
    +8/−6 %); shoulder breadth (deltoid caps move out by (k−1)·shoulder half-span, +6/−4 %). Female bodies take
    0.8 of the hypertrophy.
  - level → maturity: trunk/neck mass ×(1 +4/−3 %), a chest lift (gravitas, ≤ 0.025 head heights forward); the
    **height term is 1 by design** (joint heights plant the feet and set every slide band).
  - intelligence → brow recess ±3.5 mm (the brow ridge and the brow anchors move back; measured on the field below).
  - race (always, stats or not) → posture curve and digit distribution (below).
- **`src/game/SpineCurve.ts`** — `RACE_POSTURE` / `SUBRACE_POSTURE` / `postureCurve()` / `raceSpineColumn()`:
  human and elf erect (exact canon), dwarf lumbar ×1.6 + a 0.07 h hollow (mountain 0.08 h), ork cervical ×1.8,
  thoracic ×1.35, head 0.13 h forward (ironhide 0.15 h) on a ×1.3 trapezius (ironhide ×1.36); every term clamped
  by `POSTURE_BOUNDS`.
- **`src/graphics/body/Physique.ts`** — the inflate and carve stages, on the RENDERED skin tree only:
  `inflatePrimitive` (muscle groups, bone density, breadth, maturity; trunk muscles grow sideways by only
  `LATERAL_SHARE` = 0.35 of their gain so the bind-pose arm gap stays open — a full-x deltoid closed the ork's
  elbow-to-trunk gap from 140 mm to 0, measured), `posturePrimitive` (neck lean with a smoothstep from the neck base
  to the chin, the head rigidly forward; brow recess), latissimus sheets (strength above the reference) seated on the BACK surface behind
  the arm's plane (a sheet on the flank read as a lump between the ork's arm and ribcage, Q2 Z-46), the lumbar hollow cutter, and `landmarkCutters`
  (carve stage, scaled by the strength excess, 0 at the reference): a sternal furrow and the inguinal line from the
  iliac crest's front spine to the pubis as polylines seated on the inflated surface (a straight capsule is buried
  under a full belly and carves an inner void), and the suprapatellar groove above the patella's own front peak.
  `measureMorph` measures all of it on the skin field.
- **`src/graphics/body/Anatomy.ts`** — `anatomyFor` reads `spec.stats ?? STAT_REFERENCE` (`morphForSpec`), passes the
  morph to the sculpt, moves the face anchors with the rendered head (`morphedFace`), and returns `morph`,
  `skinInflated` (skin after inflate, before every cutter — lane P's inflate stage) and, when the stat terms are not
  the identity or the race carries a posture, `hitRoot`/`hitBounds` (the PHYSICS tree × the stat terms and the race
  posture — neck forward, trapezius; no physique sculpt, no asymmetry; the box grows by whole cells) so lane P's
  Hitboxes follow the body the player sees (Q1 Z-38: without the posture the ork's face stood 35 mm in front of its
  head capsule). `root`, `bounds`, `bones`, abductions and cell are byte-identical for every stat (G25).
- **`src/graphics/body/Hands.ts` / `HandRig.ts`** — `HandInput.digits` (length, radius, knuckle, palm multipliers;
  absent = the pre-wave hand exactly) and `HandSize.knuckleK` (MCP/PIP ring thickness); `attachHands` passes
  `plan.morph.digits`. `StatMorph.RACE_DIGITS`: elf 1.1 long / 0.88 radius / 0.94 palm; human neutral; dwarf 1.06
  radius, 1.1 knuckle, 1.03 palm (length 1.0: at 0.9 the dwarf/axe grip wrap fell to 106° < the 110° pin); ork 0.97
  long, 1.1 radius, 1.16 knuckle, 1.06 palm. Grip pins (tests/wave_w_hand_fingers_test.ts) stay green: dwarf wrap
  113–122°, ork 127–137°, elf 209–242°.

## Identity and physics

- Stats absent ≡ stats at `STAT_REFERENCE`: the same plan, byte for byte, for every race (G24); human is
  byte-identical to HEAD.
- **Deliberate, flagged for the parent:** the race terms (ork/dwarf posture on the skin; elf/dwarf/ork digits) apply
  with or without stats, so ork/dwarf skins and elf/dwarf/ork rendered hands differ from HEAD with stats absent.
  The physics of every race is HEAD's.
- The physics tree never sees the morph; only lane P's Hitboxes see it, through `hitRoot` (absent for human/elf at the
  reference, so their capsules are today's; present for ork/dwarf — their race posture — and for any non-reference stats).

## Open / honest gaps

- **Live-rig triangle budget** (fixed in r3): a stat-morphed skin gets its own cell `skinCell` = cell·√(E_morph/E_plain)
  (sign-change counts at the coarse cell on one box), capped at `SKIN_CELL_MAX_GAIN` = 1.12 × cell and set ONLY when the
  stat terms are non-identity (no-stats bodies keep today's grid, so every pre-wave pin is measured on the same mesh).
  Lane P's BodyMesh/Pipeline polygonise the skin at it. Before r3 the heavy ork at strength + level 1000 reached a 12 742 /
  12 758 live rig; the "live rig tris" column of the strength table is the r3 measurement (budget < 12 000).
- Pre-existing (HEAD): elf/high male carries two 8-vertex brow-tip islands (the pointed brow's tip is thinner than
  the grid where the socket cutter thins it). A field-level "severed tip" re-seat did not see it (the field is
  connected, only Surface Nets separates it) and flooring the tip radius at 0.7 cells did not fix it either; left
  open, allowed in G36.
- Crouched contact clips (dash/defense) seat dwarf/ork skins a few mm deeper than HEAD+K (table above): the contact
  settle plans on bone spheres, the posture moved the skin. A fix belongs in the settle (skin-aware) or a smaller posture.
- Ork and dwarf carry no readable kneecap at the reference (the thigh profile rises monotonically from the patella);
  the carve gives them one above the reference only.
