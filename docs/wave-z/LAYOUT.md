# Wave Z — lane F (Fighters layout): measured report

Generated by code from `artifacts/qa-wave-z/fighters/report.json` (tools/probe-wave-z-fighters.ts, real Chrome, build
`BUILD_OUT=dist-fighters`, real clicks/taps) and `artifacts/qa-wave-z/fighters/audit/audit.json` (tools/probe-ui-all.ts on the
same build). No number below is typed by hand.

## What changed

- **Panel head removed**: the roster section no longer has the "Chosen hero — Figure, record, statistics." head (it keeps an
  `aria-label`). The card itself is the first thing under the tabs.
- **Hero card = its picture** (`heroPlateHtml`): the PortraitStudio image (lane G: 3:4 opaque JPEG) fills a 3:4 plate edge to edge
  (`object-fit: cover; object-position: 50% 35%`), the **LV badge** sits in the plate's top-left corner, and the **name + tags**
  (race, sub-race, class, sign with its icon, weapon with its icon) and the record float over the plate's foot on a scrim.
  ≥ 768 px the plate is sticky beside the statistics; phones stack plate → action bar → sheet.
- **Roll** (`heroRollButtonHtml`): "Reroll hero" → **Roll**, purple stone (`--stone-purple` = the wave Y stone recipe on a violet
  ramp, bronze rim), dice 1.68em (1.4 × the old 1.2em), padding 3px 12px 3px 7px (was 8px 14px), still ≥ 44 px tall. Same hook
  (`data-hero-reroll`), same engine action id `reroll`, same Undo.
- **LevelBadge.ts (C-LV)**: `levelTier` (1-4 bronze, 5-8 copper, 9-12 silver, 13-16 gold, 17-20 platinum over SpectateGen's
  MIN/MAX_LEVEL, clamped, floored) and `levelBadgeHtml` (`LV` then the number). Metals: new `--metal-silver/-gold/-platinum` stacks
  beside wave Y's bronze/copper; each tier sets rim, face and ink. Used on the hero plate, the + New plate and (small) on the tabs.
- **Stat sheet placed (C-SHEET, lane S)**: `statSheetHtml(derivedSheet(character), { compact: innerWidth < 600 })` in the hero card's
  statistics column and in the + New column (`#creator-sheet`), then `paintTones`. Groups flow in balanced CSS columns (≥ 15.5rem
  each); the compact sheet packs two row-columns per group when its own column is 22-31rem wide (container query).
- **+ New wears the same plate**: the live viewer canvas fills a 3:4 plate (absolute, under everything; PreviewScene refits on
  `clientWidth/Height`), the nine sockets overlay its left/right edges, the LV badge sits top-left, the name + tags float at the foot,
  the weapon caption under them; the socket picker covers the whole plate. Wave X's steel sign left the viewer (race/sub-race are tags).
- **Everything else on the page**: tabs wrap instead of scrolling off-screen and tab names wrap instead of an ellipsis; the action
  bar is a 2-column grid on phones (Roll alone on its row unless Undo pairs with it, Play this hero full width); the calling's
  technique cards use as many columns as fit (three fixed columns pushed a long word past the viewport at 820 px).

## Mouse click paths

- **Roll a saved hero**: title → **Fighters** → the hero's tab → **Roll** (purple stone dice, first in the action bar beside / under
  the picture). **Undo** appears beside it; one click restores the hero.
- **Level**: Fighters → the metal **LV n** plate in the picture's top-left corner (small one on each tab). Metal = level band.
- **Statistics**: Fighters → hero tab → the column beside the picture (under it on a phone): Attributes, Pools, Offence, Defence,
  Mobility, Magic, Body, each value with its (+/-). Same sheet on **+ New** → right of the live viewer.
- **+ New**: Fighters → **+ New** tab → the viewer plate: drag the figure to turn; a socket on either edge opens its list over the
  plate (✕ / Esc closes); the name and tags at the foot follow every roll and edit.

## Honest gaps

- The plate's figure size/framing is lane G's PortraitStudio output (560×747 at DPR 1 when measured); the widest plate is
  560 CSS px, i.e. ×1.00 of the image's width (asked G on the board, Z-14, to size the default for the widest plate).
- `compact` (S's short labels) is chosen from `innerWidth` when the sheet is painted; resizing across 600 px without a repaint keeps
  the previous labels (the layout itself is container-query driven and refits).
- Menu.ts still exports wave Y's copper `levelBadgeHtml` (deprecated, not rendered): tests/wave_y_ui_test.ts SPEC Y35 pins it and is
  not lane F's file.
- The + New plate is at least 3:4 and grows with its content: at 320 px five 44 px sockets + the badge + the name/tags need more than
  4/3 of a 248 px plate, so it reads h/w 1.764 there (an `aspect-ratio` box clipped the foot under `overflow:hidden`; V Z-23 measured an
  intermediate build where the plate stretched to the grid row — fixed in r2). On a landscape phone the plate is sized from the window
  height, the weapon caption is hidden (the weapon tag names it) and the page's action bar is no longer pinned (it covered the
  viewer's feet band, V Z-35): **Enter the ring** is one scroll away there.
- The name/tags scrim covers the plate's bottom ~30%; on + New the figure's feet sit under it (still visible through the gradient),
  and dragging to turn works everywhere except over the scrim/sockets.
