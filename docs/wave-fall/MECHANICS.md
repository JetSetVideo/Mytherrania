## The collision law (C-FORCE, `resolveCollision(a, b, normal?)`)

1. **Stability** k = strength·agility·level·stamina·health factors × GUARD_BRACE (guarding) ÷ DOWNED_KNOCKBACK (downed), clamped
   [STABILITY_MIN, STABILITY_MAX]: strength √(s / STRENGTH_REF) ∈ [0.6, 1.5]; agility 1 + AGILITY_GAIN·(ag − AGILITY_REF) ∈ [0.6, 1.5];
   level 1 + LEVEL_GAIN·(lv − 1) ∈ [1, 1.5]; stamina STAMINA_FLOOR + (1 − STAMINA_FLOOR)·stamina01; health HP_FLOOR + (1 − HP_FLOOR)·hp01.
2. **Effective mass** m_eff = (body + armour + weapon kg) · k. Body mass = `Physics.bodyFromStats` (42 + 3.5·STR + 1.4·VIT + 1.1·RES);
   weapon mass = `WeaponSpec.mass`; **armour mass = ARMOUR_KG_PER_POINT × Stats.armor** — the game has no armour items yet (equipment is
   a placeholder), so the armour rating (class/resilience + the batch gear tier) is the proxy: armour 1 ≈ 2.5 kg padding, 4 ≈ 10 kg mail, 8 ≈ 20 kg plate.
3. **Exchange** along the contact normal: J = (1 + RESTITUTION)·μ·v_c, μ = m_a·m_b/(m_a + m_b); Δv_a = −J/m_a·n, Δv_b = +J/m_b·n
   (Σ m_eff·Δv = 0 exactly; mirror inputs give mirror outputs).
4. **Recoil** = the post-contact speed away from the other body (walking is written into x/z, so the recoil is what slides);
   predicted pushback = recoil / PUSH_DECAY (stone's 1.2 + 1/stop decay).
5. **Stagger** = clamp(STAGGER_PER_DV·max(0, |Δv| − STAGGER_DV0) / recover, 0, STAGGER_MAX), recover = agility·√strength·level·√stamina.
6. **Balance loss** = clamp((|Δv| − BALANCE_DV0)/(BALANCE_SPAN·k), 0, 1) (0 when downed); a blow's knockback speed costs
   clamp((v − HIT_BALANCE_DV0)/(HIT_BALANCE_SPAN·k), 0, 1).
7. **Triggers** — a CHARGE: in contact (≤ spacing + CONTACT_SKIN) and closing ≥ CHARGE_MIN_CLOSING with BOTH bodies closing ≥ CHARGE_EACH_MIN
   (or one alone ≥ DASH_CHARGE: a dash/lunge); a CLASH: the blades met (`Clash.detectClash`), each body driven CLASH_SPEED·strength toward
   the other. One exchange per COLLISION_COOLDOWN.

## The fall machine (C-FALL)

`stand → stagger | stumble | falling`; `stagger → stand | stumble | falling`; `stumble → stand | falling`; `falling → down`; `down → rising`;
`rising → stand | falling`. Only `stand` acts; `rising` may guard; `down`/`rising` are DOWNED. Balance loss ≥ FALL_THRESHOLD falls, ≥
STUMBLE_THRESHOLD stumbles (it ends in a fall when the seeded roll < fallChance(loss, agility, stamina)), ≥ STAGGER_THRESHOLD (or a
stagger time) staggers; stamina ≤ EXHAUSTION_STAMINA adds EXHAUSTION_BALANCE (cause "exhaustion" when that tipped it).

**Get-up law** `riseSeconds(armourMass, bodyMass, agility, hp01, stamina01)` = RISE_BASE·√(mass/RISE_MASS_REF)·(1 + RISE_ARMOUR·armour)
/ agilityFactor · (1 + RISE_WOUND·(1 − hp)) · (1 + RISE_TIRED·(1 − stamina)), clamped [RISE_MIN, RISE_MAX]; down = DOWN_SHARE·T, rising = the rest;
falling lasts FALL_SECONDS.

**Edge rule**: ring-out (`RingOut.checkRingOut`) still decides a body really outside (beyond the outline by its footprint). A body at an OPEN
brink (centre within TEETER_MARGIN inside, or beyond by less than its footprint) moving outward faster than TEETER_SPEED teeters: balance
loss TEETER_BALANCE + TEETER_PER_SPEED·(v − TEETER_SPEED) → a stumble that agility/stamina recover or not. **Back-step**: moving backward
(away from the enemy) with the rear foot over a drop deeper than the step height or on a solid prop → BACKSTEP_BALANCE. **Ledge**: pushed
(knock > 1 m/s) over a floor drop deeper than the step height inside the arena → a fall.

**Downed penalties** (down or rising): damage taken × DOWNED_DAMAGE_TAKEN; crit chance + DOWNED_CRIT_BONUS (Impact and rollStrike paths);
status/stun durations × DOWNED_STATUS_DURATION; knockback × DOWNED_KNOCKBACK (and stability ÷ it).

## The fall-aware AI (C-AI)

`CombatView` carries `selfFall` / `enemyFall` (phase, t01, downed, unbalanced, cause), `lastCollision` (ago, both pushbacks, own balance
loss), `selfStability` / `enemyStability` (effective mass) and `selfStamina01` — filled by Engine.#botFight and MatchSim alike. Decisions:
**punish** (`AiBrain.fallDecision`, wrapped round the tree by `fallAwareTree`): enemy falling / stumbling / down / rising → close in, strike when
ready, wait in reach otherwise; **edge safety** (`fallSafeDecision`): no retreat toward an open rim < FALL_EDGE_BACK_M behind while shaken (a
collision < FALL_SHAKEN_S ago, or unbalanced) or low on stamina (< FALL_LOW_STAMINA) → flank; **no tired head-on charge** into an enemy
whose effective mass is > FALL_HEAVY_RATIO × own → flank; **guard while rising** (the rising seat blocks when the enemy is in reach).

Measured (`FallStudy.fallAwareStudy`, 3000 design points × 2 passes — seat A fall-aware vs the previous brain, then seat B — counting only
matchups where both seats consult a brain; both seats play the Wave FALL rules):

| variant | aware win rate (decisive) | ± 1 SE | decisive | hits/s on a DOWNED enemy (aware / unaware) | hits/s on a STANDING enemy (aware / unaware) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| fall-aware (shipped) | 0.5106 | 0.0098 | 2605 | 0.617 / 0.554 | 0.403 / 0.406 |
| no-op control (aware off in both passes) | 0.5000 | 0.0167 | 898 | 0.546 / 0.546 | 0.396 / 0.396 |

The aware seat lands 11 % more hits per second on a downed enemy than the previous brain (standing-enemy rates equal within 0.003/s), but the WIN rate moves by 1.1 SE — not significant at 2 SE.

## Telemetry (C-LOG)

Every collision (one event per body), fall and rise goes to `FightTelemetry.force(ForceEvent)`: `{ t, kind, who, cause, phase, impulse,
balance, pushback, duration, hp01, stamina01, outcome }`; downed seconds and damage taken while downed via `downed()` / `damageWhileDown()`.
`forceSummary()` → `{ collisions, falls, rises, downS, meanDownS, damageWhileDown, causes, events (≤ FORCE_EVENT_CAP), dropped }`.
MatchReport carries it compacted (`REPORT_FORCE_EVENTS` events, numbers to 0.01, under REPORT_BYTE_CAP); MatchHistory records carry it with
`HISTORY_FORCE_EVENTS` events (500 records live in localStorage); the headless sim's `MatchOutcome.forces` (ForceTally) and `SimEvent`
kinds `collision` / `fall` / `rise`; `Summary.forceAggregate` → `BatchSummary.forces` (falls per match, mean down time, damage while down, causes).

## Sim vs engine (documented approximations)

- MatchSim has no terrain relief: the **ledge** trigger and the drop half of the **back-step** never fire there (the obstacle half does: a
  retreat pushed out of a solid prop); the edge rule uses the sim's radial rim.
- A **clash** in the sim is two wind-ups landing in the same 1/30 s tick within reach (no blade geometry); the engine uses the real blade-on-blade
  contact (`#clashTick`), which fires far more often in a mirror melee (the probe log shows ~1/s for two Warriors) but mostly with
  balance loss 0 (pushback 0.04–0.43 m).
- The sim measures velocity from position change per tick; the engine from its smoothed (~0.1 s) measured velocity.
