## Reviewer notes (judgement, dermatology / game design)

- Scar stages follow wound-healing physiology: inflammatory (0-4 d, red, swollen, moist/glossy), proliferative (4-21 d, pink granulation), remodelling (3 w-1 y, erythema fading with tau 75 d), mature (>= 1 y: paler than skin, desaturated, flat or atrophic, shiny, hairless). Checked monotone on 616 ages x 6 kinds (S1, S2).
- Hypertrophic vs keloid: hypertrophic scars stay inside the wound border and regress within ~1-2 y (burn peak 1.5 mm at ~105 d); keloids spread past the border (x1.6) and persist. M added the hypertrophic branch on QD's request (Z-24 -> Z-43, S5b).
- Langer's lines: trunk transverse at the front, oblique on flanks and over scapulae; neck, pelvis (Pfannenstiel) and the dorsum of hand/foot transverse; long limb segments longitudinal (Moore: tension lines spiral longitudinally on the limbs) turning transverse over the elbow/knee creases; forehead horizontal; elsewhere on the face radial from the orifices (crow's feet). Note: RSTL (Borges) differ on the limbs (transverse); the game follows Langer as the brief asks.
- Keloid propensity by ancestry is a fantasy mapping of the real order-of-magnitude spread between ancestries; ear-lobe piercing is correctly the top site. Ork rates are an identity choice (stated in MARKS.md).
- Tattoo ink: carbon black most stable and drifts blue-green; red/ochre fade fastest; line spread 0.08 -> ~0.68 mm over decades (dots merge, script closes up). Ink ages in biological years (elves slower).
- Marks under clothing must be hidden, not printed on the cloth (QD Z-37): M's coveredRegions + R's skin mask. A scar colour must be computed over the SKIN, never over the palette/garment colour (QD Z-61).
- Game readability (third-person camera fov 50, <= 5.5 m to the player, ~8 m to the opponent at 800 px): 6.4 / 9.3 mm per pixel. Tattoos (18-150 mm) read at fight distance (97 % >= 2 px narrow extent); scar LINES (1.5-6 mm wide) are sub-pixel there and read in the Creator close-ups (0.84-1.85 mm/px). R's shader prefilters sub-pixel strokes by area (no shimmer by construction).
- Mature-scar height ruling (closing round, Z-115): an ORDINARY laceration at 400 d must be flat or slightly atrophic (M: -0.049 mm) - a raised ordinary scar is a FAIL; a HYPERTROPHIC one may still be +0.4-0.5 mm and faintly pink at 13 months (rise from weeks 4-8, peak 3-6 months, regress over 1-2+ years; model 1.5 mm peak at 120 d, 0.44 mm at 400 d, ~0 at 730 d) - acceptable, do not speed regression. A keloid never regresses and is never 'pale'. While hypertrophic activity >= 0.1 the stage must not be called 'mature: pale, flat'; captions must say 'hypertrophic' when the resolved flag is true.
