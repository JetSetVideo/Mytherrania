## What renders

Every skinned body material (`CelSkinned.createCelSkinnedSurfaceMaterial({ material })`, `#define USE_WAVE_TEX`) reads:

| channel | source | shader effect |
|---|---|---|
| roughness | `aMaterial.x` (vertex), decal lerp (fragment) | r < 0.5: cel highlight band on a Phong lobe, exponent mix(6, 48, g), band softness mix(0.2, 0.05, g), peak 0.3 x light, g = 2(0.5 - r); a wider rim (edge 0.5, 0.1 g). r > 0.5: the rim fades to 40 % at r = 1. 0.5 = the pre-TEX look. |
| height | `aMaterial.y` (m, per-triangle gradient) + decal relief (bind-frame finite differences) | perturbed normal (perturbNormalArb on screen derivatives), tilt clamped at MAX_TILT = 0.3 rad; the lambert change also modulates the albedo x 0.9 (clamped 0.7..1.3) so relief reads inside a flat band |
| ao | `aMaterial.z` (P's cavity share x geometric occlusion, MeshOcclusion) | multiplies the whole lit term (ambient + bands + rim + spec + fill); not emissive |
| edge crest | `aEdge` (vertex, optional; P's masks.crest) | per-fragment smoothstep of the interpolated weight lifts the albedo toward white (`USE_WAVE_TEX_EDGE`) |
| subsurface | `aMaterial.w` | warm wrap at the terminator: base x (1, 0.42, 0.28) x light x smoothstep(-0.35, 0, n.l)(1 - smoothstep(0, 0.3, n.l)) x 0.35 x sss |
| emissive | `aEmissive` (vertex, optional) + decal albedo x emissive | added after lighting |
| marks | fragment decals (<= 16) | albedo normal-over, roughness lerp, relief, emissive; hidden under garments (`aSurface.x`, the preset's skin mask) |

Dead zones (neutral): |r - 0.5| < 0.004, ao > 0.998, subsurface < 0.002, |emissive| < 0.0009765625, height == 0.

### The lit path is compiled only when something is off-neutral

`USE_WAVE_TEX_LIT` is defined when the vertex channels are off-neutral, an emissive attribute exists, or a decal is set (`MaterialRender.setDecals` toggles it, one recompile). Without it the fragment runs the pre-TEX statement verbatim (`celShade(baseColor * vPaint, ...) * detailK`).
Why: three attempts that kept the old arithmetic inside a richer function (a re-typed copy with guarded terms; `celShade(...)` plus guarded terms; an early `return celShade(...)` beside the extended path) all moved 8-9 pixels by 1/255 in Chrome/ANGLE-Metal — the GPU compiler re-associates (fast math) once more terms share the value. Only the separate compile is bit-exact.

## Fragment decals (layout posted on the board Z-14 / Z-19)

`uniform vec4 uDecals[112]` = 16 decals x 7 vec4, `uDecalCount`, `uDecalRelief`, `uGlyphAtlas` (R8 256x256, 13 Symbols glyphs, distance field).

| vec4 | x | y | z | w |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| D0 | center.x | center.y | center.z | bounding radius |
| D1 | tangent.x | tangent.y | tangent.z | halfLength |
| D2 | normal.x | normal.y | normal.z | halfWidth |
| D3 | albedo.r (linear) | albedo.g | albedo.b | albedoOpacity |
| D4 | kind | glyph (glyph/dots/band) or stroke count | blur (m) | seed 0..1 |
| D5 | roughness | roughnessOpacity | height (m) | emissive |
| D6 | spacing (m) | wrapRadius (m) | wrapMode (0 tangent, 1 bitangent) | glyphs in a row |

Kinds: 0 line, 1 stitched, 2 disc, 3 glyph, 4 dots, 5 band. Coverage = 1 - smoothstep(-a, a, s), a = sqrt((0.7 px)^2 + blur^2), px = |dFdx(vBind)| + |dFdy(vBind)| (bind metres per pixel); a stroke thinner than a pixel keeps its area (coverage x clamp(2W/px, 0.25, 1)). Each decal: early reject by its bounding sphere, facing weight smoothstep(0.15, 0.45, n.N), one atlas fetch for glyph kinds. Relief: a plateau of the decal height with steep shoulders, half width max(min(0.35 W, 1.5 mm), blur, px/2) (closing round F; the first law spread it over max(W, blur, px) and a 0.4 mm scar barely tilted the normal); three evaluations (p, p + e t1, p + e t2, e = max(px/2, 0.15 mm)) only where a relief decal's sphere contains the fragment. Stitched decals draw one centred line (count = stitches; QC REN-9). Glyph kinds (glyph / dots / band) carry no relief (the packer zeroes their height), so a glyph costs exactly one atlas fetch per fragment it touches (QC Z-89).

## WebGL limits

- three r180 is WebGL2-only: GLSL ES 3.00 fragment shaders always have highp (decal geometry in bind metres needs it; mediump would quantise 1 m to ~0.5 mm).
- Fragment uniforms: 158 vec4 rows incl. three's prefix, against the guaranteed 224 (TX33, conservative count: every scalar a row); samplers 2 of 16.
- Loops have constant bounds (16 decals, 8 strokes) with an early break on uDecalCount; no derivative inside the decal functions (TX39).

## Wiring (TexWiring.ts; SkinnedHumanoid default path)

`options.material` (explicit) wins; else `texMaterialFor`: P's `presetFor(subjectOfSpec)` + `applyPresetOverrides(appearance.layerOverrides)` -> `presetBuffers` (albedo replaces the v1 paint, aMaterial, masks.skin -> aSurface.x) and M's `marksForProfile(…, spec.seed)` -> `resolveMarks(regionFrames(mesh))` -> `marksToDecals({ mesh, skinSrgb: appearance.body, skinRoughness: mean skin roughness })`. `TEX_DEFAULTS = { generatedMarks: true, preset: true }` decides what an appearance without explicit marks/overrides gets; both false + no explicit marks/overrides = neutral (identity above). `rig.material.stack` reports what was evaluated (C's HUD).

## Material relief details (P's FragmentDetail)

`USE_WAVE_TEX_DETAILS`: up to 4 materials x 2 details (`uniform vec4 uMatDetail[8]` = kind, period m, amplitude m, octaves) with a one-hot `aDetailW` vertex weight per material (blends across seams). Drawn kinds, all procedural from the bind position (no texture fetch), each fading out as its period nears the pixel footprint: **pores** (sparse dimples), **weave** (twill on the facing plane), **grain** (fbm, octaves), **hammered** (shallow dents), **strands** (noise stretched along the bind up axis), **plates** (jittered lattice, sunken seams), **studs** (domes, radius 0.16 period, on the facing plane). Features with a period < 20 mm send 30 % of their bump to the band normal and the rest through the albedo (+-20 %, as wave X); coarser ones (studs) are full bumps under the tilt bound. When P's details are present the generic wave-X skin grain / cloth twill gains are set to 0 (abs relief kept).

Fine-texture meter (close-ups, front final view, mean |luma Laplacian| over body pixels, outlines excluded): ork-female spine: details on 2.782 / off 0.981; human-male shoulderL: details on 1.878 / off 0.98; dwarf-male spine: details on 2.421 / off 1.018; elf-female head: details on 2.635 / off 1.81.

## Known limits

- Hands (HandRig, static CelShader meshes), face parts, hair and weapons are not channel materials: debug views show them in their final colours, and marks never reach the fingers.
- In the preset path the hands keep the v1 skin paint (no finish), so a glazed skin can differ slightly at the wrist.
- Vertex height (`aMaterial.y`) gives a per-triangle gradient: at the 22-35 mm cell its normals are soft/faceted, so relief claims rest on decals (scars) and P's fragment details, not on vertex height (L Z-87).
- The Layers inspector's edits reach the render through P's `applyPresetOverrides` on the preset (ids `p:<material>.<step>`), not through `evaluateStack(presetToV2)`: the inspector stack is a view, the game path is `presetBuffers` (L Z-87, QC PRE-5).
- Inside the lit branch celShade's terms are evaluated once (one shadow lookup; closing round F: the second 9-tap PCF lookup was most of the +1.3 ms arena cost); the band AA width there is fwidth(n.l) x the shadow term, taken in uniform flow (fwidth(light) would include the shadow edge but must not be taken after the neutral branch).
- Generated marks often land under the class outfit and are hidden (correct, but a Warrior shows few marks); captures need bare-skin anchors for torso/thigh marks.
