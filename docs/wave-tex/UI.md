## What the user gets (Fighters -> + New, under the viewer: **Surface**)

- **Channel view row** (radio group, arrows/Home/End): Final · Albedo · Roughness · Height · Normal · AO · Marks — R's `rig.material.setChannelView`
  (a uniform switch, no rebuild). The GEN stage scrubber and the channel view work together: picking a channel view while a non-Paint
  generator stage is shown brings the scrubber to **7 Paint** (material channels exist on the finished body only); picking a stage keeps
  the chosen view and the note under the row says it shows on 7 Paint.
- **Scars & tattoos**: + Scar / + Tattoo, the list (select, Delete key or × to remove), and the editor of the selected mark: kind (scar) or
  style + motif + ink + accent ink (tattoo), region (+ side for paired parts), **Place on the model** (then click or drag on the 3D body:
  the viewer's raycast returns the skin region + M's frame anchor), Around/Along (the anchor by keyboard), size, width, age, angle with
  *Follow skin lines* (Langer's lines, scars) / *Upright* (tattoos), Mirror. Each mark row shows M's validator notes (overlap, runs off
  the body part, over the eyes/mouth) and **hidden under the outfit** when the region is a garment slot (kept and saved, drawn when bare).
  An unsaved fighter shows M's GENERATED marks; the first edit keeps them as the user's own (`appearance.marks`).
- **Material layers**: the active stack (P's preset finish layers, bottom first), filtered by material (Skin by default, All, Hair,
  each garment slot): On/Off, a row opens its **opacity** and one **operator stepper per channel** (only the operators P's game path can
  run, `editableOps`; safety steps `*.clamp`/`*.guard` are listed locked), reorder by **dragging the ⠿ grip** (mouse or finger) or with
  **ArrowUp/ArrowDown** on the grip, **Reset to preset**. Edits are sparse `appearance.layerOverrides` (ids `p:<material>.<step>`).
- Marks and overrides are saved with the hero (Save as profile) and change the preview key only when present (pre-wave keys identical).

## Click paths (mouse; a finger does the same)

- **See a channel**: title -> **Fighters** -> **+ New** -> under the viewer, **Surface** -> the row **Final · Albedo · Roughness · Height · Normal · AO · Marks**.
  Arrow keys move along the row. On a generator stage other than 7 Paint, a channel view switches the scrubber to 7 Paint.
- **Add and place a scar**: + New -> **Scars & tattoos** -> **+ Scar** (placing turns on) -> click the body where it goes (drag on the body to slide it;
  a drag off the body still turns the figure) -> set Kind, Length, Width, Age, Angle or *Follow skin lines*, Mirror -> **Esc** or the button stops placing.
- **Add a tattoo**: same panel -> **+ Tattoo** -> click the body -> Style, Motif, Ink, Accent, Size, Age, Upright/Angle, Mirror.
- **Remove**: the × beside a mark (or Delete on the selected row).
- **Edit the layers**: + New -> **Material layers** -> Material (Skin, Hair, each garment) -> **On/Off**; click a layer's name for **Opacity** and the
  operator steppers (‹ multiply ›); drag the **⠿** grip up/down (or focus it and press ArrowUp/ArrowDown); **Reset to preset** undoes every edit.
- **Keep it**: **Save as profile** (the bar at the bottom) — the marks and layer edits are saved on the hero.

## Honest gaps

- The operator pickers offer what P's game path runs (`editableOps`); which of those make a VISIBLE difference depends on the step's
  top colour/alpha (P's evaluateFinish) — an operator change can be invisible on a step whose top equals the base.
- Layer and mark edits rebuild the body (~0.1-0.6 s) on each committed step; a placement DRAG moves the reticle live and rebuilds on release.
- Marks on a garment region are kept and saved but not drawn (R hides decals under cloth): the panel says 'hidden under the outfit'.
- The anchor is computed on the bind pose of the clicked triangle (barycentric); a click on the posed body lands within one triangle of the
  cursor (the idle pose moves the body a few mm).
- A saved hero cannot be reopened in + New for editing (no such path exists in the Fighters page); the round trip is proven on the stored
  record and by rebuilding the preview from it (same key, same frozen picture).
